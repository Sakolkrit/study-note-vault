# ELMo 2018

> **In one line for a 12-year-old:** In an old word list, 'bank' has one meaning. ELMo gives each word a different meaning depending on the sentence around it.

## The problem
A word means different things in different sentences, but fixed word vectors give it one vector.

## What people did before
Fixed word vectors such as [[Word2Vec 2013]], one vector per word.

## The idea
Word vectors that are learned functions of the internal states of a deep bidirectional language model pre-trained on a large text corpus ([[Pre-training]]).

## What they found
The abstract says these representations can be added to existing models and significantly improve the state of the art across six challenging NLP problems, including question answering, textual entailment and sentiment analysis. It adds that exposing the deep internals of the pre-trained network is crucial (abstract).

## Why it mattered
It popularised 'pre-train on plain text, then reuse' in language tasks, just before [[BERT 2018]] (from memory).

## Limits and open questions
The vectors are added on top of other models, which are still built separately for each task (from memory). BERT then fine-tuned the whole network.

## Concepts used
[[Embedding]], [[Recurrent Neural Network]], [[LSTM]], [[Transfer Learning]], [[Pre-training]]

## Reading trail
- **Came from:** [[Word2Vec 2013]]
- **Led to:** [[BERT 2018]]
