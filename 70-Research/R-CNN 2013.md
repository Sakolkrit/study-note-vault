# R-CNN 2013

> **In one line for a 12-year-old:** First suggest boxes where an object might be. Then let a picture-recognising network look inside each box and say what it is.

## The problem
Say what objects are in a picture and where, not only what the whole picture is.

## What people did before
Complex ensembles of hand-made image features; performance on PASCAL VOC had plateaued (abstract).

## The idea
Run a high-capacity [[Convolutional Neural Network]] on bottom-up region proposals, and when labelled data is scarce, pre-train on an auxiliary task then fine-tune ([[Transfer Learning]]).

## What they found
mAP of 53.3% on VOC 2012, more than 30% relative improvement on the previous best. It also beat OverFeat on the 200-class ILSVRC2013 detection set (abstract).

## Why it mattered
It made CNNs the base of detection too.

## Limits and open questions
Slow, because the network runs on each proposed box separately (from memory; Fast R-CNN's abstract gives the speedups: 9x faster training and 213x faster testing).

## Concepts used
[[Image Classification]], [[Transfer Learning]], [[Fine-tuning]], [[Object Detection]]

## Reading trail
- **Came from:** [[AlexNet 2012]]
- **Led to:** [[Fast R-CNN 2015]], then [[YOLO 2015]]
