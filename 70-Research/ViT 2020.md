# ViT 2020

> **In one line for a 12-year-old:** Cut the picture into little squares, treat each square like a word, and let a Transformer read them.

## The problem
Does vision need convolutions at all?

## What people did before
Convolutional networks such as [[ResNet 2015]], with attention sometimes added on top.

## The idea
A pure [[Transformer]] on a sequence of image patches ([[Vision Transformer]]).

## What they found
When pre-trained on large data and transferred to ImageNet, CIFAR-100 and VTAB, it attains excellent results against the best convolutional networks while needing substantially fewer computational resources to train (abstract).

## Why it mattered
Showed one architecture could serve text and images.

## Limits and open questions
Needs a lot of pre-training data to shine (the abstract's condition 'when pre-trained on large amounts of data').

## Concepts used
[[Transformer]], [[Self-Attention]], [[Pre-training]]

## Reading trail
- **Came from:** [[ResNet 2015]]
- **Led to:** [[CLIP 2021]] (uses it as one of its two image encoders)
