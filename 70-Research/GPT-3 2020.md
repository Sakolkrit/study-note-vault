# GPT-3 2020

> **In one line for a 12-year-old:** A very big next-word predictor that can do new tasks just from a few examples in the prompt, without being retrained.

## The problem
Avoid collecting thousands of labelled examples for every new task.

## What people did before
Pre-train then fine-tune per task ([[BERT 2018]]).

## The idea
Scale an autoregressive language model to 175 billion parameters, 10x more than any previous non-sparse model, and give tasks and examples purely as text ([[In-Context Learning]]).

## What they found
Strong few-shot results on translation, question answering and cloze tasks, and on-the-fly tasks like unscrambling words or 3-digit arithmetic, sometimes competitive with fine-tuned systems. Human judges had difficulty telling its news articles from human ones. The abstract also names datasets where it struggles and contamination from web data (abstract).

## Why it mattered
Showed that scale unlocks few-shot learning. Prompting became a way to use models ([[Prompt Engineering]]).

## Limits and open questions
Struggles on some datasets, and the abstract notes training-on-the-test issues from large web corpora ([[Benchmarks]]).

## Concepts used
[[LLM]], [[Scaling Laws]], [[In-Context Learning]]

- **Led to:** [[InstructGPT 2022]], [[Chinchilla 2022]], [[LLaMA 2023]], [[LoRA 2021]] (adapts GPT-3 175B)
