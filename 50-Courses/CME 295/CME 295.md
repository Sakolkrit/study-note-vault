## How the lectures fit together
1. **Lectures 1 and 2: the machine.** Text becomes numbers, the Transformer reads it with attention, and BERT shows the encoder side.
2. **Lecture 3: the big decoder.** Large language models, how they pick words, and how they are prompted and sped up.
3. **Lectures 4 to 6: shaping the model.** Pretraining and cheap fine-tuning (4), steering towards human preferences (5), and training models to reason (6).
4. **Lectures 7 and 8: using it.** Retrieval, tools and agents (7), then how to evaluate all of it (8).
5. **Lecture 9: looking out.** Images, diffusion for text, and open problems.
